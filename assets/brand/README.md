# Creator OS — Totem Cut brand identity

**Status:** Selected logo (2026-11-03).

Totem Cut is an abstract three-layer symbol: charcoal outer layers express structure, and the violet center represents creative transformation. The identity avoids stock play-button and AI-sparkle motifs. The approved concept artwork was raster; these SVG masters are a geometric, editable reconstruction, not a pixel-for-pixel auto-trace.

## Authoritative files

- `totem-cut-symbol.svg`: transparent primary mark.
- `totem-cut-app-icon.svg`: dark rounded-square icon master.
- `totem-cut-lockup.svg` and `totem-cut-lockup-on-dark.svg`: horizontal wordmarks.
- `totem-cut-monochrome-black.svg` and `totem-cut-monochrome-white.svg`: single-color marks.
- `app-icon-*.png`: QA renders from 16px through 1024px (generated).
- `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/`: PNG, ICO, ICNS required by desktop packaging (generated).

## Brand palette
- Charcoal `#14151A`
- Violet `#8B7CF6`
- Off-white `#F7F6F4`
- White `#FFFFFF`

## Brand rules
1. Do not stretch, skew, rotate or apply drop shadows to the symbol.
2. Maintain a clear area at least one-quarter of the symbol width on all sides.
3. Prefer 1-color artwork in single-color print and low-contrast use.
4. Keep the complete wordmark for large signage and the standalone app icon for UI.
5. Verify legibility at 16px and 24px (small-scale test sheet).
6. The SVG wordmark text uses system typeface fallbacks; choose and outline a licensed typeface before registration or print production.
7. This work is not trademark clearance. Conduct clearance before filing or broad commercial rollout.

Brand geometry and UI tokens are separate concerns. Interface colors remain in `packages/ui/src/tokens.css`.
